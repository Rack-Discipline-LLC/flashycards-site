FlashyCards Privacy Policy

Updated November 2026 · Copyright Rack Discipline LLC 2026

Contact

For all privacy concerns and questions, contact us at info@rackdisciplineusa.com.

The FlashyCards app

FlashyCards works with the text you type in, such as your cards and decks. There is no account or sign-in, and the app does not send your content to any server of ours. Your content is kept on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced across your own devices (see “Data stored in iCloud” below). The app works with text only: it does not use your camera, photos, or microphone, and it uses no advertising, analytics, or outside services.

This notice also covers our websites, including flashycards.rackdisciplineusa.com.

General

This privacy notice for Rack Discipline LLC describes how and why we might collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your information when you use our services ("Services"), such as when you:

  • Visit our website at rackdisciplineusa.com, or any website of ours that links to this privacy notice
  • Download and use our application(s), such as FlashyCards — or any other application of ours that links to this privacy notice

Children's privacy

FlashyCards is an educational app that children may use for learning. The app has no accounts, collects no personal information, contains no advertising, and includes no third-party analytics or tracking, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Anything a child enters stays on their device and in their own iCloud account. Our websites are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question or concern, contact us at info@rackdisciplineusa.com.

Questions or concerns?

Reading this privacy notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at info@rackdisciplineusa.com.

Key points

  • What personal information do we process? When you visit, use, or navigate our Services, we may process personal information depending on how you interact with Rack Discipline LLC and the Services, the choices you make, and the products and features you use.
  • Do we process any sensitive personal information? We do not process sensitive personal information.
  • Do you receive any information from third parties? We do not receive any data about you from third parties.
  • Do you share any personal information with third parties? We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for their own purposes. Period.

What information do we collect

  • Personal information you disclose to us. We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you contact us. The FlashyCards app has no accounts.
  • Personal information provided by you. The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the Services. It may include:
    • Email address
    • Other contact information
  • Files you import. If you choose to import cards from a CSV or Anki-format file, the app reads the file you select, on your device, to create cards. The file is not sent to us. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.
  • Data stored in iCloud. The text you enter in FlashyCards is kept on your device and can sync across your devices using your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit). That data lives in your iCloud account, not on our servers, and we cannot see it. Apple's privacy policy applies to how iCloud stores and handles it.
  • Your control. Your cards and decks are entirely under your control. You can edit or delete them in the app at any time, and you can remove the data stored in iCloud through your device's iCloud settings. We hold no copy of your content, so there is nothing for us to delete or retain.
  • Payment data. FlashyCards is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we do not receive or collect your payment details. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for more information.
  • Social media logins. We do not work with, or share data with, social media platforms. Period.
  • Information collected automatically. Some information — such as your IP address and/or browser and device characteristics — is collected automatically when you visit our Services. This information does not reveal your specific identity but may include device and usage information. It is only used to maintain the security and operation of our Services, and for our internal analytics and reporting purposes.
  • Cookies and analytics. Our websites, including our blog at blog.rackdisciplineusa.com, use cookies to remember preferences and to make the sites work. They also use Google Analytics to collect usage statistics: which pages are visited, how visitors reach us, the type of device and browser, approximate location (country or city), and which links are clicked, such as the link to the App Store. We use this to understand how the sites are used and to improve them. We have turned off Google's advertising features in Analytics, we do not use this data for advertising, and Analytics data is kept for 14 months. We exclude visits from our own devices. Analytics stays off until you click Accept in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the page footer. We set one cookie of our own, to remember your choice for 180 days. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's privacy policy, and you can opt out with Google's browser add-on. We never sell data to third parties or use it to target advertising. Our apps do not include advertising or third-party analytics.
