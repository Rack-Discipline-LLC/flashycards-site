FlashyCards

A self-learning app — flashcards and study flow, built for learning at your own pace. Coming soon to the App Store — the download link goes live at launch.

Learn it once, remember it for good

FlashyCards is a self-paced flashcard app built around spaced repetition: you see each card just before you would forget it. Study anything you can write down — vocabulary, kanji, capital cities, maths facts — with your cards kept on your device and synced through your own iCloud.

Review at your own pace

See the prompt, think of the answer, then tap to check. Your daily review shows how many cards are due, so you always know where you stand.

Tell it how well you knew it

Rate each answer Again, Hard, Good or Easy. FlashyCards schedules the next review for you — cards you know come back less often, and cards you struggle with come back sooner.

Any subject, any language

Cards aren’t limited to one kind of content. Study languages, geography, maths, or anything else you can put into words — including kanji and other non-Latin scripts.

Your whole collection, easy to find

Browse everything you’re learning in one list, search it, and filter by tag to focus on one subject.

Build cards your way

Each card holds several fields — for example kanji, reading and meaning — with alternate answers where more than one is right. Choose which direction to test, one way or both, and organise cards with tags.

Set the pace that suits you

Choose how likely you want to be to remember a card when it comes due, cap the longest gap between reviews, and set daily limits for new cards and reviews so studying stays manageable.

Bring your cards with you

Import cards from a CSV file or an Anki file, and export or back up whenever you like. Update items by tag, reset your review progress, or delete a group of cards — with a backup saved first.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your cards stay on your device and sync through your own iCloud — we never see them. Read the privacy policy.
